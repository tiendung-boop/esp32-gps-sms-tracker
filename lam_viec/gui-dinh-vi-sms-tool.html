San sang.

Moi chu ky, trang se tu mo app Nhan tin voi so + noi dung toa do da dien san — ban chi can bam Gui trong app do (trinh duyet khong duoc phep tu gui SMS ngam vi ly do bao mat cua he dieu hanh).
