====================================================================
 HE THONG GUI + NHAN VI TRI QUA SMS (ESP32) - KHONG CAN WIFI/INTERNET
 Goi dung cho may Wyse 3040 hoac Wyse 5070 (Windows 10 IoT Enterprise)
====================================================================

TONG QUAN
---------
He thong gom 2 con ESP32 rieng biet, noi voi nhau chi bang SMS (khong can
WiFi/internet o ca 2 dau):

  [ESP32 GUI]  --GPS + SIM 4G--> SMS -->  [ESP32 NHAN] --USB--> [May Wyse nay]
  (gan xe/vi tri can theo doi)          (cam vao may Wyse)      (chay web xem ban do)

  1. ESP32 GUI (o hien truong, khong WiFi): doc GPS, cu 5 phut gui 1 tin SMS
     dang "Toa do: <lat>,<lon>" toi 1 so dien thoai co dinh (SIM gan trong
     ESP32 NHAN).
  2. ESP32 NHAN (cam USB vao may Wyse): tu doc SMS den qua module SIM, in ra
     cong USB dang "TOADO|deviceId|ten|lat|lon".
  3. May Wyse chay 2 chuong trinh Node.js:
     - 1 server web local (127.0.0.1, KHONG can internet) hien ban do.
     - 1 script doc du lieu tu cong USB cua ESP32 NHAN, day vao server o tren.
     Mo trinh duyet vao trang do se thay vi tri cap nhat song song.

QUAN TRONG - WYSE 3040 vs WYSE 5070
------------------------------------
2 dong nay khac phan cung dang ke:
  - Wyse 5070: CPU Intel Gemini Lake/Core i3-i5, RAM thuong >=4GB - hau het
    cai Windows 64-bit. Dung bo cai trong cai-node-js/x64/.
  - Wyse 3040: CPU Intel Atom x5-Z8350, chi 2GB RAM - dong nay hay duoc Dell
    cai san Windows 10 IoT Enterprise BAN 32-BIT de nhe may, du chip ho tro
    64-bit. PHAI kiem tra truoc, dung nham bo cai se khong cai duoc.
  Cach kiem tra tren tung may: mo PowerShell, go:
      (Get-CimInstance Win32_OperatingSystem).OSArchitecture
  Ket qua hien "64-bit" -> dung thu muc cai-node-js/x64/.
  Ket qua hien "32-bit" -> dung thu muc cai-node-js/x86/.
  (Hoac vao Settings > System > About, xem dong "System type".)
  Wyse 3040 chi co 2GB RAM - he thong nay nhe (server + script Node vai
  chuc MB RAM) nen van chay duoc, nhung tranh mo them nhieu ung dung nang
  khac cung luc tren may do.

===========================================================
BUOC 1 - NAP FIRMWARE CHO 2 CON ESP32 (chi lam 1 lan/moi con)
===========================================================
Can may tinh co cai Arduino IDE (khong nhat thiet la may Wyse - co the nap
tu may khac roi mang ESP32 da nap xong toi day).

1a. ESP32 GUI (firmware-esp32/GpsSmsSender/GpsSmsSender.ino)
    - Phan cung: ESP32 + module GPS (NEO-6M) + module SIM 4G A7680C + nguon
      rieng cho module SIM (3.4-4.2V, chiu dong dinh ~2A - KHONG lay tu chan
      3.3V/5V/VIN cua ESP32).
    - File secrets.h di kem da co san so dien thoai nhan (SIM gan trong ESP32
      NHAN). NEU doi sang SIM nhan khac, phai sua dong PHONE_NUMBER trong
      GpsSmsSender/secrets.h truoc khi nap, dinh dang quoc te vd "+84987654321"
      (KHONG so 0 dau).
    - Thu vien can cai qua Library Manager: TinyGPSPlus.
    - Nap xong, dat ESP32 nay o vi tri can theo doi (co nguon rieng, KHONG
      can cam vao may tinh nao nua).

1b. ESP32 NHAN (firmware-esp32/SmsReceiverUSB/SmsReceiverUSB.ino)
    - Phan cung: ESP32 + module SIM 4G A7680C (SIM nay la so dien thoai da
      dien trong PHONE_NUMBER o buoc 1a). KHONG can GPS.
    - Khong can thu vien them, khong can secrets.h.
    - Day noi module SIM -> ESP32 (giu nguyen theo comment dau file .ino):
        VBAT/VCC  -> nguon ngoai rieng 3.4-4.2V (KHONG lay tu ESP32)
        GND       -> GND chung voi ESP32
        TXD       -> GPIO16 (ESP32 RX2)
        RXD       -> GPIO17 (ESP32 TX2)
        PWRKEY/RST -> de troi, khong noi gi
    - Nap xong, con ESP32 nay se LA con cam USB vao may Wyse khi dung thuc
      te (day USB vua cap nguon vua truyen du lieu ve may tinh).
    - (Tuy chon) Neu muon ban do hien ten rieng (vd "Xe may 1") thay vi so
      dien thoai tho, KHONG can sua code/nap lai firmware nua - dung trang
      "Thong tin thiet bi" o Buoc 3 ben duoi (danh cho nguoi dung hang ngay,
      khong can biet code). Cach sua thang KNOWN_SENDERS trong
      SmsReceiverUSB.ino (vi du comment san trong file) van con dung duoc
      nhung it dung hon vi phai nap lai firmware moi lan doi ten.

===========================================================
BUOC 2 - CHUAN BI MAY WYSE - CHI LAM 1 LAN, KHONG CAN INTERNET
===========================================================
Toan bo phan nay KHONG can internet - bo cai Node.js va thu vien can thiet
da duoc tai san va dong goi ngay trong thu muc nay roi.

2a. Cai Node.js (ban LTS v22.23.2 "Jod"):
    - Truoc tien xac dinh may Wyse nay la 64-bit hay 32-bit (xem muc "QUAN
      TRONG - WYSE 3040 vs WYSE 5070" o dau file).
    - Vao dung thu muc cai-node-js/x64/ (hoac x86/ neu may 32-bit), bam dup
      file .msi trong do.
    - Cai dat nhu binh thuong (Next > Next > Install) - bo cai se tu them
      "node" va "npm" vao PATH cua Windows, khong can chinh gi them.
    - Kiem tra da cai xong: mo PowerShell MOI (phai mo cua so moi de PATH
      cap nhat), go:
        node -v
      Neu hien "v22.23.2" la OK.
    - Neu may khong co quyen admin de chay .msi, dung ban .zip portable
      trong cung thu muc do thay the - xem muc "KHONG CO QUYEN ADMIN" o
      cuoi file nay.

2b. Thu vien serialport (cho script doc USB): KHONG can lam gi ca - thu muc
    may-nhan-SMS/node_modules/ da duoc dong goi san (bao gom file nhi phan
    .node cho ca Windows x64 va x86), sao chep nguyen thu muc "Wyse 5070"
    nay sang may la dung duoc ngay, khong can chay "npm install" nua.

2c. Driver USB-serial cho ESP32 NHAN (chi can neu Windows KHONG tu nhan ra
    cong COM khi cam ESP32 vao - xem chi tiet cach kiem tra o muc "XU LY SU
    CO" ben duoi):
    - Board ESP32 dung trong du an nay dung chip CP2102 (Silicon Labs) de
      chuyen USB <-> serial. Bo driver chinh hang da tai san trong
      driver-cp2102/CP210x_Windows_Drivers.zip - KHONG can internet.
    - Cach cai: giai nen file zip do, vao dung thu muc theo kien truc may
      (CP210xVCPInstaller_x64.exe cho may 64-bit, _x86.exe cho may 32-bit -
      xem lai muc "QUAN TRONG - WYSE 3040 vs WYSE 5070" o dau file), chay
      file .exe do, cai nhu binh thuong. Can quyen admin.
    - Cam lai ESP32 sau khi cai xong (rut ra cam lai, hoac restart may neu
      van khong len).
    - Neu board dang dung lai la loai chip KHAC (vd CH340 thay vi CP2102 -
      xem cach phan biet o muc "XU LY SU CO"), bo driver nay se KHONG dung
      duoc - can tim driver CH340 rieng (chua co san trong bo nay, can
      internet 1 lan tu 1 may khac de tai roi mang qua).

===========================================================
BUOC 3 - CHAY HANG NGAY (khong can lam lai Buoc 1, 2 nua)
===========================================================
1. Cam ESP32 NHAN (da nap SmsReceiverUSB.ino) vao may Wyse bang day USB.
2. Vao thu muc may-nhan-SMS, bam dup (hoac "Run") file:
       nhan-sms-tu-dong-esp32-usb.bat
   - No tu mo 1 cua so server ban do (cua so nay GIU MO trong luc dung, dong
     cua so nay la tat toan bo he thong).
   - Roi no hoi "Nhap cong COM cua ESP32": neu chua biet cong nao, cu bam
     Enter de trong -> no tu liet ke danh sach cong COM dang co (vd COM5,
     COM7...) -> chay lai file .bat, lan nay go dung ten cong (vd COM5).
   - Cong ESP32 thuong la cong MOI xuat hien khi vua cam day USB vao (kiem
     tra Device Manager > Ports (COM & LPT) neu can chac chan). Neu chua co
     driver CP210x/CH340, Windows co the khong nhan ra cong - cai driver
     tuong ung roi cam lai.
3. Khi cong mo thanh cong, cua so nay se hien "Da mo cong COMx... dang doc
   SMS tu dong". Trinh duyet cung se tu mo sang trang ban do.
4. Cho ESP32 GUI (o hien truong) gui 1 tin SMS moi toi. Trong vong toi da
   20 giay, cua so script se hien dong "Nhan toa do: ... -> lat, lon", va
   marker se hien/cap nhat tren ban do trong trinh duyet.
5. MUON DUNG HAN: dong ca 2 cua so (cua so server + cua so script doc USB),
   hoac dong cua so may (ca 2 se tu dong theo neu mo tu 1 cua so cmd chung).

DAT TEN THIET BI (hien ten rieng thay vi so dien thoai tren ban do)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Tren trang ban do (nhan-sms-tu-dong-tool.html), o cot trai co nut "Thong tin
thiet bi" - bam vao se mo 1 tab moi la trang danh ba so dien thoai/ten
thiet bi. Dien so dien thoai (so ESP32 GUI/dien
thoai dung de gui SMS toa do) va ten muon hien (vd "Xe may 1"), du lieu tu
luu lai NGAY va ban do se tu doi ten thiet bi do trong lan cap nhat vi tri
tiep theo (khong can khoi dong lai gi ca). Co the dien tay tung dong, hoac
bam "Nhap tu Excel (.xlsx)" de nhap hang loat (cot 1 la so dien thoai, cot 2
la ten). LUU Y: trang nay PHAI mo qua dung nut/link tren (tuc la qua server
dang chay o 127.0.0.1:8899) thi du lieu moi len duoc ban do - neu bam thang
file chay-thong-tin-thiet-bi-tool.bat (mo truc tiep bang file, khong qua
server) thi trang van dung duoc nhung chi luu rieng tren trinh duyet do
(pill "Chi luu tren may nay" thay vi "Dong bo voi ban do"), KHONG hien len
ban do.

CACH THAY THE - dung dien thoai Android thay vi ESP32 NHAN
------------------------------------------------------------
Neu khong co san ESP32 NHAN + module SIM ma co san 1 dien thoai Android
(SIM nhan gan trong dien thoai do), co the bo qua het Buoc 1b va dung:
    nhan-sms-tu-dong-dien-thoai-adb.bat
Can chuan bi truoc (xem chi tiet comment dau file doc-sms-qua-dien-thoai-adb.js):
  1. Cai "Android Platform Tools" (co adb.exe), them vao PATH.
  2. Tren dien thoai: bat "Tuy chon nha phat trien" > "Go loi USB" (USB
     debugging), cam USB vao PC, bam "Cho phep" khi dien thoai hoi.
LUU Y: cach nay CHUA duoc kiem chung tren thiet bi that (chi test bang du
lieu gia lap khi viet script) - neu bi loi "Permission denied" hoac khong
doc duoc SMS moi du dien thoai co tin nhan, day la gioi han cua ROM/Android
(mot so may chan quyen doc content://sms/inbox qua adb), khong phai loi cu
phap - quay lai dung cach ESP32 NHAN (on dinh hon, da test).

XU LY SU CO THUONG GAP
===========================================================
- Cam ESP32 NHAN vao may nhung KHONG THAY cong COM nao moi xuat hien (kiem
  tra: mo PowerShell, go lenh sau, TRUOC va SAU khi cam ESP32 de so sanh:
      [System.IO.Ports.SerialPort]::GetPortNames()
  hoac mo Device Manager > Ports (COM & LPT)):
  1. Trong Device Manager, xem co muc nao nam duoi nhom "Other devices" voi
     dau cham than vang khong -> co nghia la Windows thay thiet bi nhung
     CHUA co driver dung. Bam vao muc do xem ten chip (thuong ghi "CP210x"
     hoac tuong tu) de xac nhan dung loai truoc khi cai driver o Buoc 2c.
  2. Neu Device Manager KHONG hien gi ca du da cam (khong co "Other
     devices", khong co gi thay doi truoc/sau khi cam) -> nhieu kha nang la
     DAY USB chi sac, khong co day du lieu (rat hay gap voi day di kem
     board ESP32). Thu doi sang day khac chac chan co truyen du lieu (vd
     day tung dung de nap firmware qua Arduino IDE).
  3. Neu da chac chan la thieu driver (buoc 1 xac nhan) va dung chip CP2102
     -> lam theo Buoc 2c o tren (driver da co san trong driver-cp2102/,
     khong can internet).

- "Khong mo duoc cong COMx" khi chay script:
  Co chuong trinh khac (thuong la Arduino IDE Serial Monitor) dang giu cong
  do. Chi 1 chuong trinh giu duoc 1 cong COM tai 1 thoi diem - dong het
  chuong trinh kia roi chay lai .bat.

- Da thay dong "Nhan toa do..." nhung web khong hien marker:
  Kiem tra cua so server (nhan-sms-tu-dong-server.js) co con mo khong / co
  bao loi gi khong. Trang web tu poll lai moi 6 giay - F5 lai trang de chac
  chan dang xem du lieu moi nhat.

- Nghi ngo day noi module SIM sai / muon xem phan hoi AT command tho:
  Nap tam firmware-esp32/ATBridge/ATBridge.ino (cong cu rieng, cho phep go
  lenh AT thu cong qua Serial Monitor de kiem tra module SIM con song
  khong, SIM co nhan dien khong...). Nap lai SmsReceiverUSB.ino sau khi
  kiem tra xong.

- ESP32 GUI gui SMS bao loi mang (vd "+CMS ERROR: Network timeout") o MOI
  lan gui, du song/SIM binh thuong khi dung tren dien thoai:
  Nhieu kha nang la nha mang (vd Viettel) tam khoa gui SMS do SIM vua doi
  sang thiet bi moi (xac thuc "doi may"), CAN goi hotline/app nha mang de
  xac thuc lai - khong phai loi code/day noi.

- ESP32 tu reset (thay dong chu "ets Jul 29 2019...") khi vua cam USB hoac
  vua mo cong COM: BINH THUONG, khong phai loi - cac chip USB-serial tren
  board (CP2102/CH340) tu dong reset ESP32 moi khi co chuong trinh moi mo
  cong. Cu de no chay tiep, khong can can thiep gi.

- Cai file .msi bao loi/khong chay tren Wyse 3040:
  Kha nang cao la dung nham kien truc (cai ban x64 tren may dang chay Windows
  32-bit). Kiem tra lai bang lenh PowerShell o muc "QUAN TRONG - WYSE 3040
  vs WYSE 5070" dau file, roi chuyen sang dung thu muc cai-node-js/x86/.

KHONG CO QUYEN ADMIN TREN MAY (khong chay duoc file .msi)
===========================================================
Dung ban portable (giai nen, khong can cai dat/khong can quyen admin) - cung
da tai san, nam CUNG thu muc voi ban .msi (x64/ hoac x86/ tuy may):
  1. Giai nen file .zip trong do ra bat ky dau, vi du C:\node-portable\
     (sau khi giai nen se co dang C:\node-portable\node-v22.23.2-win-x64\node.exe
     hoac ...-win-x86\node.exe tuy ban da giai nen).
  2. Trong cac file .bat o may-nhan-SMS/, sua chu "node" thanh duong dan day
     du toi node.exe vua giai nen, vi du dong:
        node nhan-sms-tu-dong-server.js
     sua thanh:
        "C:\node-portable\node-v22.23.2-win-x64\node.exe" nhan-sms-tu-dong-server.js
     (sua ca 2 cho co chu "node " trong tung file .bat, va truoc "node
     doc-sms-qua-esp32-usb.js" trong PowerShell/cmd khi chay tay).
  3. Cach nay khong can chinh PATH he thong (vi khong co quyen admin thi
     thuong cung khong sua duoc PATH he thong), chi can duong dan day du la
     chay duoc.
